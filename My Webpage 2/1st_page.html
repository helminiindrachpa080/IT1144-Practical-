The University of Vavuniya is a public university located in the district of Vavuniya, Sri Lanka. Established in June 2021, the university was formed by the Government of Sri Lanka through the conversion of the Vavuniya Campus of the University of Jaffna into the University of Vavuniya. The university offers a four-year degree program, the Bachelor of Science Honours in Environmental Science, and several other degree programs aligned with the Outcome-Based Education and Learner-Centered Teaching (OBE-LCT) framework and the Sri Lanka Qualification Framework (SLQF) guidelines. The university is coeducational and officially recognized by the University Grants Commission of Sri Lanka. University of Vavuniya
